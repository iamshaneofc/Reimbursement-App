Page 3 — Business Rules
Centralize money and workflow logic; do not scatter rules across UI components
1. Approval matrix
Estimated / claimed value
Required business approvals
≤ ₹25,000
Reporting Manager
₹25,001–₹75,000
Reporting Manager → Head of Department
₹75,001–₹2,00,000
Reporting Manager → Head of Department → Head of Division
> ₹2,00,000 or international
Reporting Manager → Head of Department → Head of Division → MD/CEO
Every claim
Finance verification after business approvals
2. Approval edge cases
Approver cannot approve their own claim.
If claimant is the Reporting Manager for a level, skip that level and route to the next required level.
Approvers may return with remarks; returned claims go back to the employee for correction and resubmission against the same Travel Request ID.
Rejected claims are terminal for the current request unless the product explicitly supports resubmission as a new version; do not silently reopen them.
Every decision should create an audit event.
3. Advance rules
Advance may be up to 60% of estimated employee-borne cost.
Advance is adjusted against the final settlement.
If final claim < advance, amount recoverable from employee = advance − net reimbursable claim.
Payable and recoverable must be mutually exclusive.
4. Expense policy rules
Policy area
Rule
Lodging
Tier 1 room tariff ≤ ₹6,000/night; Tier 2 ≤ ₹4,000; Tier 3/other ≤ ₹2,800. Taxes on room tariff are fully reimbursable. Excess tariff is disallowed, not silently removed.
Air travel
Domestic economy; centrally booked and company-paid; employee does not claim it.
Meals
Tier 1 ₹1,500/full day; Tier 2/below ₹1,000/full day. Actuals up to limit. Bills required above ₹500.
Local conveyance
Actuals against receipt; airport transfers covered.
Business entertainment
Separate category. Attendees + organisation required. HOD prior approval required when > ₹2,000.
Non-reimbursable
Laundry, minibar, in-room entertainment, spa, gym, personal phone/data, fines/penalties, independent insurance, and expenses incurred by someone other than claimant.
5. Submission / proof / payment
Settlement must be submitted within 7 calendar days of return.
Every claim line requires a supporting proof reference.
Duplicate submission is a policy breach; reconcile by bill number, date, amount, merchant where available.
Finance payment runs occur on the 10th and 25th of each month. Prototype may model payment status rather than real transfer.

4. Security rules
Every mutating endpoint performs authentication and authorization server-side.
Employee can access only own requests/settlements.
Manager can act only on claims routed to them.
Finance can access finance queue and payment actions.
Never trust a client-supplied role, employeeId, approvalStepId, or eligible amount.
Eligible/disallowed totals must be calculated on the server from source expense values and policy rules.
Prevent invalid status transitions even if an API is called directly.
